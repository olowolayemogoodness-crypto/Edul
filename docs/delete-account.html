Delete Your Edulink Account

You can permanently delete your Edulink account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do this, depending on whether you still have the app installed.

Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Edulink app and sign in
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Under Account Actions, tap Delete account
  4. Confirm your password when prompted
  5. Tap Delete forever

This immediately and permanently deletes your posts, comments, likes, follows, notifications, and your account itself.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or are unable to sign in, email us to request deletion:

Email: olowolayemogoodness@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your Edulink account so we can locate and remove it. We will confirm once your data has been deleted.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (login credentials)
  • Your profile information (name, university, course)
  • Your posts, comments, and voice notes
  • Your likes, reposts, and follow relationships
  • Your study data (streaks, quiz history, XP)
  • Notifications addressed to you
This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

See our full Privacy Policy for more detail on how your data is used before deletion.
